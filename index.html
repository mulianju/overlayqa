FAQ

Does the extension upload my mockups?扩展会上传我的设计稿吗？

No. Images and layer settings stay in Chrome storage and IndexedDB on the device. A license key is the only product data sent out, and it goes to your license Worker.

不会。图片和图层设置留在这台设备的 Chrome storage 和 IndexedDB 里。唯一会发出去的产品数据是许可密钥，而且只发给你的许可 Worker。

Why can the extension read sites?为什么扩展能读取网站？

The overlay has to be drawn on the page. The script does nothing until you enable OverlayQA on that origin. It does not ship analytics.

叠层必须画在页面上。在你为该来源启用 OverlayQA 之前，脚本不会做任何事。扩展不带分析。

What is Pro today?现在的 Pro 包含什么？

Figma import and split view work. Breakpoint guides, notes on an exported screenshot, and folders are included. Window-resizing presets are guides, not a resized browser.

Figma 导入和分屏已经可用。断点参考线、导出截图上的批注和文件夹也包含在内。断点不会真的改变浏览器窗口大小，而是画出参考线。
